Postdoctoral Fellow

Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen

I am a postdoctoral fellow at Harbin Institute of Technology (Shenzhen). My research focuses on video analysis, embodied intelligence, and model compression. I aim to build efficient AI systems that provide human-centered services with less computation and hardware overhead.

I serve as a reviewer for conferences and journals including ICCV, CVPR, AAAI, TNNLS, TMM, KBS, and SPL.

Research

My work currently centers on three related directions:

  • Video Analysis: efficient video recognition, temporal action detection, and compact video representations.
  • Embodied Intelligence: visual perception and reasoning for agents that interact with physical environments.
  • Model Compression: data-free knowledge distillation and resource-efficient model learning.
